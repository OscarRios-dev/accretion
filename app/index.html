Obsidian folders drawn in the galaxy. Removing a vault never deletes anything on disk.

You can also drag a folder onto the window.

Ignored folders

One per line. Matching folders and notes never appear (e.g. Templates, Private/, *.excalidraw.md).

View

Accretion is read-only and works offline: it never modifies your notes and never sends them anywhere.
